# Grill me

Interview me relentlessly about every aspect of this plan until we reach a shared understanding.

Walk down each branch of the design tree, resolving dependencies between decisions one by one:

1. Ask **one question at a time**. Wait for my answer before moving on.
2. For each question, give **your recommended answer** and a short reason, so I can just accept it or push back.
3. If a question can be answered by exploring the codebase or project files, **explore it yourself** instead of asking me.
4. Follow up on vague or hand-wavy answers. Point out contradictions, missing edge cases, hidden assumptions and risks.
5. Keep a running list of decisions made and open questions.

Stop when every branch is resolved, then give a concise summary of:

- Decisions taken
- Remaining open questions or risks
- Suggested next steps
